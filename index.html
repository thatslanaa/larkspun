Saved on this device

Songs you make are saved in this browser only.

Everything runs in your browser. Your recording and your songs are never uploaded; they stay on this device until you delete them here or clear the browser's site data.

Songs are generated by an AI system from your hum and description, and every exported file says so in its metadata (EU AI Act, Article 50). Instruments, beats and lyrics are synthesised or written by Larkspun itself; no samples or third-party recordings are used.
